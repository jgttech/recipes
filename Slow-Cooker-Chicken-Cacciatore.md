# Slow Cooker Chicken Cacciatore

**Source:** Well Plated by Erin Clarke
**URL:** https://www.wellplated.com/slow-cooker-chicken-cacciatore/
**Prep:** 15 minutes | **Cook:** 4 hours | **Servings:** 4–6

## Ingredients

- [ ] 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil, plus 2 teaspoons (divided)
- [ ] 2 pounds boneless, skinless chicken breasts or thighs
- [ ] 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- [ ] ½ teaspoon ground black pepper
- [ ] 1 medium yellow onion, chopped
- [ ] 3 cloves garlic, minced
- [ ] 1 tablespoon balsamic vinegar, plus ½ teaspoon (divided)
- [ ] 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
- [ ] 1 medium green bell pepper, chopped
- [ ] 8 oz sliced baby bella (cremini) mushrooms
- [ ] 2 teaspoons Italian seasoning

### For Serving

- [ ] Whole wheat pasta, brown rice, polenta, rice, zucchini noodles, or shredded spaghetti squash
- [ ] Chopped fresh parsley and freshly grated Parmesan cheese

## Instructions

1. **Prep the slow cooker and brown the chicken.** Lightly coat the slow cooker with nonstick spray. Heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Season both sides of the chicken with salt and pepper. Once the oil is shimmering, lay the chicken in a single layer and sear for 3–4 minutes on the first side until nicely browned (move it as little as possible). Flip and cook another 2 minutes on the second side. Transfer the chicken to the slow cooker.

2. **Sauté the aromatics.** Lower the skillet heat to medium and wipe it out with a paper towel. Add the remaining 2 teaspoons of olive oil. Once hot, add the chopped onion and cook about 3 minutes until softening. Add the garlic and 1 tablespoon of balsamic vinegar, then cook for 1 more minute until the garlic is fragrant and the vinegar has reduced. Scrape everything (including pan drippings) into the slow cooker.

3. **Add remaining ingredients and cook.** Top the chicken with the crushed tomatoes, green bell pepper, mushrooms, and Italian seasoning. Cover and cook:
   - **Chicken breasts:** 1½–2½ hours on HIGH or 4–5 hours on LOW
   - **Chicken thighs:** 3–4 hours on HIGH or 5–6 hours on LOW

   Pull the chicken out as soon as it reaches 165°F internal temperature. Chop, shred, or leave the pieces whole.

4. **Thicken and finish (optional but recommended).** Uncover the slow cooker and let the sauce cook on HIGH for about 1 hour to thicken and develop flavor. Just before serving, stir in the remaining ½ teaspoon of balsamic vinegar. Taste and adjust salt and pepper. Return the chicken to the sauce if you've shredded or chopped it. Serve hot over your starch of choice with extra sauce, parsley, and Parmesan on top.

## Nutrition (per serving, 1 of 6)

228 calories | 32g protein | 10g carbs | 8g fat | 3g fiber | 6g sugar

## Toddler & Family Notes

- Since long pasta is off the table for now, this works well over rice, polenta, or mashed potatoes.
- For the toddler portion, shred the chicken finely and chop bell pepper and mushroom pieces small (or omit mushrooms — the recipe author notes you can).
- To keep sodium reasonable for an 18-month-old, halve or skip the kosher salt and let adults salt at the table.
- One reviewer specifically mentioned their 1.5-year-old loved this dish.
