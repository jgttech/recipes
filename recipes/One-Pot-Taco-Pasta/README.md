# One-Pot Taco Pasta

**Source:** Amy Palanjian, Yummy Toddler Food
**Recipe URL:** [https://www.yummytoddlerfood.com/one-pot-taco-pasta/](https://www.yummytoddlerfood.com/one-pot-taco-pasta/)
**Prep:** 10 minutes | **Cook:** 20 minutes | **Servings:** 6

## Ingredients

- [ ] 1 tablespoon olive oil
- [ ] ½ pound ground beef
- [ ] ½ cup grated or minced onion
- [ ] ½ cup crushed tomatoes
- [ ] 2 teaspoons cumin
- [ ] 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- [ ] ½ teaspoon salt
- [ ] 2 cups small pasta (elbow macaroni or similar)
- [ ] 1 quart broth (reduced-sodium preferred)
- [ ] 14.5 ounces black beans
- [ ] 2 cups sweet corn
- [ ] 1½ cups shredded cheddar cheese

### For Serving (optional)

- [ ] sour cream
- [ ] avocado
- [ ] crushed tortilla chips
- [ ] cilantro
- [ ] salsa
- [ ] hot sauce

## Instructions

1. **Brown the beef.** Warm the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat, then add the ground beef and onion. Cook for 8–10 minutes, breaking the meat apart as it browns. Drain off any excess fat.
2. **Add the seasonings.** Stir the crushed tomatoes, cumin, garlic powder, and salt into the pot.
3. **Bring the broth to a simmer.** Pour in the broth and crank the heat to high. Once it boils, dial it back to medium so the liquid simmers steadily.
4. **Add the pasta.** Tip in the pasta and stir gently to distribute.
5. **Cook until tender.** Let it simmer for about 14 minutes, until the pasta is tender and most of the liquid has been absorbed.
6. **Finish with beans, corn, and cheese.** Turn off the heat. Stir in the black beans, corn, and shredded cheddar, mixing until the cheese melts through.
7. **Serve.** Dish it up and let everyone add their preferred toppings.
