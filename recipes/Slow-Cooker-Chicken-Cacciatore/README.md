# Slow Cooker Chicken Cacciatore

**Source:** Erin Clarke (Well Plated)
**Recipe URL:** [https://www.wellplated.com/slow-cooker-chicken-cacciatore/](https://www.wellplated.com/slow-cooker-chicken-cacciatore/)
**Prep:** 15 minutes | **Cook:** 4 hours | **Servings:** 4–6

## Ingredients

- [ ] 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil plus 2 teaspoons, divided
- [ ] 2 pounds boneless skinless chicken breasts or thighs
- [ ] 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- [ ] ½ teaspoon ground black pepper
- [ ] 1 medium yellow onion chopped
- [ ] 3 cloves garlic minced
- [ ] 1 tablespoon balsamic vinegar plus 1/2 teaspoon, divided
- [ ] 1 can crushed tomatoes 28 ounces
- [ ] 1 medium green bell pepper chopped
- [ ] 8 ounces sliced baby bella cremini mushrooms
- [ ] 2 teaspoons Italian seasoning

### For Serving

- [ ] Whole wheat pasta brown rice, polenta, rice, zucchini noodles, or baked and shredded spaghetti squash
- [ ] Chopped fresh parsley freshly grated Parmesan cheese

## Instructions

1. **Sear the chicken.** Lightly spray a large slow cooker with nonstick spray. In a large skillet over medium-high heat, warm 1 tablespoon of olive oil. Season both sides of the chicken with salt and pepper. When the oil is hot and shimmering, lay the chicken in a single layer and let it cook undisturbed for 3–4 minutes to develop a good sear, then flip and brown the second side for about 2 more minutes. Move the chicken to the slow cooker.
2. **Soften the aromatics.** Lower the skillet heat to medium and carefully wipe out the pan with a paper towel. Add the remaining 2 teaspoons of olive oil, then the onion, and cook for about 3 minutes until it begins to soften. Stir in the garlic and 1 tablespoon balsamic vinegar and cook for 1 minute, until the garlic is fragrant and the vinegar has reduced. Scrape everything — pan drippings included — into the slow cooker.
3. **Slow cook with the vegetables.** Top the chicken with the crushed tomatoes, green bell pepper, mushrooms, and Italian seasoning. For chicken breasts, cover and cook on HIGH for 1½–2½ hours or on LOW for 4–5 hours; for chicken thighs, cook on HIGH for 3–4 hours or on LOW for 5–6 hours. Pull the chicken out as soon as it reaches 165°F. Optionally chop or shred it, or leave the pieces whole.
4. **Thicken and finish the sauce.** You can serve immediately, but for the best flavor, uncover the slow cooker and cook on HIGH for 1 more hour to thicken the sauce and deepen its flavor. Just before serving, stir in the remaining ½ teaspoon balsamic vinegar and adjust salt and pepper to taste. If the chicken was chopped or shredded, return it to the sauce and toss to warm through; otherwise spoon sauce generously over whole pieces. Serve hot over pasta, rice, polenta, or veggie noodles, finished with extra sauce, parsley, and Parmesan.
